Daehyun Choi

Postdoctoral Researcher  ·  Bhamla Lab, Georgia Institute of Technology

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Bhamla Lab at the School of Chemical & Biomolecular Engineering, Georgia Institute of Technology, working with Prof. M. Saad Bhamla. My research connects bioinspired propulsion, vortex dynamics, fluid–structure interaction (FSI), and machine-learning-based flow diagnostics — with the goal of translating the fluid physics of biological locomotion into next-generation underwater vehicles, soft robots, and physical AI systems.

I received my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from Seoul National University (SNU) in 2022, advised by Prof. Hyungmin Park, where I received the Best Ph.D. Thesis Award. Before joining Georgia Tech, I was a postdoctoral fellow at Ajou University (2022–2023) with Prof. Daeshik Kang.

My projects are supported by DARPA (Young Faculty Award, 2024; $500k) and the National Research Foundation of Korea (Sejong International Postdoctoral Fellowship, 2023; $140k).

     

Research interests — click to expand
  • Bioinspired underwater propulsion: squid-inspired soft nozzles, mudskipper locomotion, water-walking insects
  • Vortex dynamics and impulse/thrust enhancement in starting and pulsed jets
  • Fluid–structure interaction: flexible/compliant nozzles, elastic wave propagation, Moens–Korteweg scaling
  • Deep learning for experimental fluid mechanics: optical flow (PWC-Net), instance segmentation, pose estimation
  • Bayesian surrogate modeling and data-driven optimization for propulsion and flow control
  • Multiphase flows: liquid atomization, bubbly flows, capillary and interfacial dynamics
  • Physical AI: bridging biological intelligence, fluid physics, and robotic implementation
