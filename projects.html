Active Projects

Squid-Inspired Nozzles for Enhanced Efficiency and Thrust in Rotary Propulsors

Support: Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency (DARPA)  ·  Lab: Bhamla Lab, Georgia Tech

Sep. 2024 – present

Designing and characterizing squid-inspired compliant nozzles for rotary propulsion systems. Conducting PIV and dynamometry experiments to quantify thrust amplification mechanisms. Developing deep-learning Bayesian surrogate models for nozzle geometry optimization.

Keywords: bioinspired propulsion, FSI, deep learning, Bayesian optimization, DARPA

Related publications: arXiv:2605.03239 arXiv:2605.17319

Hydrodynamics of Water-Hopping Mudskipper and Biomimetic Robot Development

Support: National Research Foundation of Korea (NRF) – Sejong International Postdoctoral Fellowship  ·  Lab: Bhamla Lab, Georgia Tech

Sep. 2023 – present

Investigating interfacial hydrodynamics and 3D kinematics of mudskipper water-hopping locomotion through laboratory experiments and biological fieldwork (Borneo). Developing stereo-camera 3D tracking system for non-invasive pose estimation in natural habitats. Building physical and robotic models to validate discovered locomotion principles.

Keywords: bioinspired robotics, interfacial dynamics, 3D tracking, high-speed imaging

Related publications: Integr. Comp. Biol. 2025

Interface & Multiphase Dynamics

Support: Multiple (Samsung Electro-Mechanics, Daewoo Shipbuilding, SAIT)  ·  Lab: MFFV Lab, Seoul National University

2016 – 2022

Understanding mass and momentum transfer through fluidic interfaces. Topics include bubble/droplet velocimetry using CNN-based optical flow, liquid atomization by dual-nozzle gas jets, spray coating film formation, and superhydrophobic surface effects on cylinder wake. Developed DeepBubbleVelocimetry — the first CNN-based flow diagnostic tool for bubble-laden systems.

Keywords: multiphase flow, CNN, optical flow, atomization, interfacial dynamics

Related publications: Sci. Rep. 2022 J. Fluid Mech. 2022 (atomization)

Fluid–Structure Interaction: Starting Jets & Multi-body Flows

Support: Multiple (NRF, Samsung, Yonsei Hospital)  ·  Lab: MFFV Lab, Seoul National University

2016 – 2022

Investigated flow-structure interaction of starting jets through flexible nozzles. Studied multi-body wake interactions in in-line sphere arrays. Developed medical devices for urinary surgery including Foley catheter removal apparatus and hydronephrosis induction system (3 granted patents). Studied electroconvective flows in porous membrane systems.

Keywords: FSI, flexible nozzle, starting jet, sphere array, biomedical device, PIV

Related publications: J. Fluid Mech. 2022 (FSI) J. Membr. Sci. 2021 Phys. Fluids 2018
